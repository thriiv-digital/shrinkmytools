About HEIC

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the default photo format on iPhones since iOS 11 — it compresses better than JPG, but most non-Apple software, websites, and older programs can't open it, which turns "just send me the photo" into a frustrating compatibility problem.

How this works

This converter uses a WebAssembly-based HEIC decoder running entirely in your browser, so your photos are never uploaded to a server — a meaningful difference from most HEIC converters, since photos often contain sensitive personal content.

  • Choose JPG for photos and smaller file sizes.
  • Choose PNG if you need lossless quality (larger files).

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do iPhones use HEIC instead of JPG?

HEIC compresses roughly twice as efficiently as JPG at the same visual quality, saving significant storage space — Apple made it the default format in iOS 11.

Will I lose quality converting HEIC to JPG?

There's a small quality trade-off since JPG uses different compression, but at high quality settings the difference is generally not visible to the eye.

Does this work with Live Photos?

This converts the still HEIC image only — Live Photos also include a short video component that isn't part of the HEIC file itself.
