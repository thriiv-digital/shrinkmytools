About Word & Character Limits

Word and character counts matter more than people realize — school essays have minimums, job applications cap cover letters, and nearly every online platform enforces a character limit somewhere. This tool counts as you type so you're never guessing whether you're over the limit.

How reading time is calculated

Reading time is estimated at the commonly cited average adult silent reading speed of ~200 words per minute.
