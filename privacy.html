Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Shrink My Tools ("we", "the site") provides free, browser-based utilities including an image resizer, QR code generator, and PDF merge/split tool. This policy explains what data is and isn't collected when you use them.

Your files

Images, PDFs, and text you process using these tools are handled entirely within your own browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers. Once you close or refresh the tab, anything you've processed is gone unless you've downloaded it.

Analytics and advertising

We may use third-party services such as Google AdSense to display ads and Google Analytics to understand site traffic. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, and may use this data to personalize ads. You can control ad personalization via Google Ads Settings and manage cookies via your browser settings.

Third-party libraries

These tools load open-source libraries (such as pdf-lib and qrcode-generator) from public CDNs to power certain features. Loading these files may share your IP address and browser information with the CDN provider, consistent with normal web browsing.

Cookies

We do not set first-party cookies to identify you personally. Third-party ad/analytics providers may set their own cookies as described above.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address listed on the site.
