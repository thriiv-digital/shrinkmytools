About This Converter

Covers the most commonly needed conversions across five categories: length, weight, volume, area, and temperature (which uses proper formulas rather than a simple ratio, since Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin don't share a common zero point). All calculations happen instantly as you type, entirely in your browser.

  • Length: millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers, inches, feet, yards, miles
  • Weight: milligrams, grams, kilograms, tonnes, ounces, pounds, stone
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin
  • Volume: milliliters, liters, US gallons, US cups, fluid ounces
  • Area: square meters, square feet, acres, hectares, square kilometers

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the conversions?

All conversions use standard, internationally recognized conversion factors (e.g. exactly 25.4mm per inch) — results are accurate to at least 6 significant figures.

Why does temperature work differently from the other categories?

Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin don't share a common zero point, so temperature can't use a simple multiplication factor like length or weight — it uses the correct offset formulas instead.

Can I convert between categories, like weight to volume?

No — unit conversion only works within a single category (e.g. length to length), since converting between fundamentally different physical quantities requires knowing the substance's density, which this tool doesn't ask for.
