About Shrink My Tools

Shrink My Tools is a small collection of free, browser-based utilities built for everyday tasks — resizing images, generating QR codes, merging PDFs, formatting JSON, and more — without needing to sign up, install anything, or upload your files to a server.

Why it's different

Most "free" online tools work by uploading your file to a server, processing it there, and sending it back. That means your files pass through someone else's infrastructure, and there's always a wait for the upload and download. Every tool on Shrink My Tools runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly — nothing is ever uploaded, so there's no wait and no privacy trade-off.

The tools

  • Shrink My Pics — resize and convert images to WebP, JPG, or PNG
  • QR Code Generator — create and download custom QR codes
  • PDF Merge, Split & Compress — combine, split, or shrink PDF files
  • JSON Formatter & Validator — beautify, minify, and validate JSON
  • Word & Character Counter — live text stats and reading time
  • Case Converter — UPPERCASE, camelCase, snake_case, and more
  • Unit Converter — length, weight, temperature, volume, area
  • Password Generator — strong, random passwords with a strength meter
  • Hash Generator — MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512 hashes
  • Color Picker & Converter — HEX, RGB, HSL, plus palettes
  • Loan & Mortgage Calculator — monthly payments and amortization
  • Lorem Ipsum Generator — placeholder text for design mockups
  • HEIC to JPG/PNG Converter — convert iPhone photos, batch supported
  • Favicon Generator — every icon size from one image
  • Watermark Adder — add text watermarks to one or many photos
  • BMI Calculator — metric or imperial units
  • Percentage Calculator — percentages, increase, decrease, and change
  • Age Calculator — exact age or days until any date
