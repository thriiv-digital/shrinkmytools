Why Shrink My Tools?

Most "free" online tools quietly upload your files to a server to process them — which means your documents and images pass through someone else's infrastructure before you get them back. Every tool here runs entirely in your own browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly, so your files are never uploaded, never stored, and never seen by anyone but you. No accounts, no watermarks, no limits.
