About These Calculations

  • "What is X% of Y" — e.g. calculating a tip, a discount amount, or a commission.
  • "X is what % of Y" — e.g. figuring out what portion of a budget was spent, or a test score.
  • "% change from X to Y" — e.g. tracking growth or decline between two values over time; shows a negative result when the value decreased.
  • "Increase/decrease by X%" — e.g. applying a price increase, a raise, or a discount directly to a number.

All calculations update instantly as you type — nothing is sent anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between "percentage change" and "percentage points"?

Percentage change is relative (going from 10% to 15% is a 50% increase), while percentage points is the raw difference (a 5 percentage point increase) — this calculator computes percentage change.

Why is my "% change" result negative?

A negative result means the second number is smaller than the first — i.e. a decrease rather than an increase.

Can I use this for calculating a tip?

Yes — use the first calculator ("what is X% of Y") with your bill total and desired tip percentage.
