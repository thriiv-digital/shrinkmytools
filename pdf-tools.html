    About Merging & Splitting PDFs

    Combining and splitting PDFs are two of the most common document tasks — merging separate scanned pages or reports into a single file to send, or pulling specific pages out of a larger document without re-scanning anything. Both are simple structural operations (no re-rendering or quality loss involved), which is exactly why they can run entirely in your browser instead of needing a server.

    How it works

    This tool uses a WebAssembly-powered PDF library to read each file's internal page structure, then copies the selected pages into a new PDF document — byte for byte, with no re-compression or quality loss. Text stays selectable, images stay at full resolution, and formatting is preserved exactly as it was in the original file.

    Tips

    • When merging, files are combined in the order shown in the list — use the up/down arrows to reorder before merging.
    • Page ranges for splitting are 1-indexed (page 1 is the first page) and support both individual pages and ranges, e.g. 1-3,5,8-10.
    • Password-protected PDFs can't be processed until the password is removed first.
