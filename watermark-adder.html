About Watermarking

A watermark deters casual image theft and reinforces attribution when photos are shared or reposted — commonly used by photographers, small businesses listing products, and anyone sharing preview images publicly before a sale or full release.

  • Tiled watermarks are much harder to crop out than a single corner mark, at the cost of being more visually intrusive.
  • Lower opacity (40-60%) keeps the underlying image visible while still being clearly present.
  • This tool processes everything locally — your original photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add a logo image instead of text?

This version supports text watermarks only — a logo/image watermark option may be added in a future update.

Does the watermark reduce image quality?

No — the original image is unchanged apart from the watermark overlay; output is saved as PNG, so there's no additional compression loss.

What opacity should I use?

40-60% is a good default — visible enough to deter casual reuse, but not so strong it obscures the photo underneath.
